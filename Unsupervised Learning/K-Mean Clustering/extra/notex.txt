K-Means is a clustering algorithem used to group data into k distinct non-overlapping subgroups --> Unsuperviesed = No labels are used (no y) --> Find natural accuring patterns in data with no relevence to labels 

The algorithem minimizes intra-cluster distance (distance within each cluster)

How it works:
    1. Choose k clusters centers randomly 
    2. Assign each data point to the nearest center
    3. Move the cluster centers to the mean of assigned points 
    4. Repeat until convergence (centers dont move much) 

Terms:

    Centroid: Center of a cluster
    Inertia: Sum of squared distances of samples to their closest cluster center - lower is better 
    k: number of clusters
