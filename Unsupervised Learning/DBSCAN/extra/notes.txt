Density-Based Spatial Clustering of Applications with Noise also known as DBSCAN discovers clusters of arbitrary shape based on density --> Automatically detects outliers

eps (E): Maximum distance between two samples to be considered in the same neighborhood 

min_sample: Minimum number of samples required to form a dense region 

How it works:

    Core point has at least min_samples points within eps radius 

    Border point: Within eps of core point but not core point

    Noise point: Not close enough to any core points 

Advantages
    * No need to specifiy number of clusters 
    * Can find arbitrarily shaped clusters 
    * Detects outliers naturally 

Disadvantages 
    * Sensitive to eps and min_samples 
    * Not ideal for datasets with varying densities
